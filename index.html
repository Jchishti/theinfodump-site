What is The InfoDump?

The InfoDump is an independent studio that makes useful, privacy-respecting software — apps and tools we wanted to exist and built ourselves.

We don't track you. We don't sell your data. We don't run ads. We just make stuff and won't shut up about it until we get it right.

If that sounds like your speed, say hi.

JC
Founder

Jamal Chishti

Builder, designer, and the reason you can't get him to shut up about software.

The InfoDump is a one-person studio out of Oregon. Jamal built Linear Timer+ because he needed it, then kept building it because other people said they did too. He's late-diagnosed AuDHD (Autistic + ADHD) — which mostly translates to an obsessive eye for detail, a pile of half-formed project ideas, and a pace that lurches between sprint and standstill. The good ideas will, eventually, end up on this page as real things you can use.

He believes software should be useful, honest, and quiet: no tracking, no ads, no dark patterns. If a tool can't earn its keep without those, it shouldn't exist.
